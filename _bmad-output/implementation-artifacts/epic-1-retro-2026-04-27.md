## What Went Well

- **Code review culture established.** 4 adversarial reviews caught 6 real patches — `forbidOnly` in Playwright config, `fastify-cli` missing from devDependencies, `timeout-minutes` absent from E2E job, dual reporter, fixture import path, and more. Zero false positives inflating the list.
- **`@shared/types` multi-runtime resolution.** TypeScript, Vite, and Node.js `node:test` all resolve `@shared/*` correctly across three different module systems. Zero rework required in later stories.
- **Deferred-work log maintained discipline.** 20 items logged with reasons — not everything became a patch. Signal over noise.
- **Known gaps resolved promptly when they became blocking.** fastify-cli and test:shared were deferred in 1-4, then pulled into 1-6 exactly when CI needed them.

## What Didn't Go Well

- **Scaffolding tool gaps drifted through multiple stories before surfacing as blocking.** `fastify-cli` and `test:shared` were identified in story 1-4's review, deferred, and lingered until story 1-6 — 2 stories later. No trigger condition documented to prompt earlier resolution.
- **Story spec referenced a Playwright API (`test.todo`) without version-validating against the installed package.** Discovered at implementation time, required a workaround (`test.fixme`). Created friction and wasted a lookup cycle.

## Root Cause

Both challenges share the same root cause: **assumptions encoded early, not validated until blocking.** Early feedback loops were missing for both scaffolding tool assumptions and library API availability.
